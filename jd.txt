Job Title: Junior Software Engineer / Software Analyst
Company: TechSolutions BD
Location: Dhaka, Bangladesh (Hybrid)

Job Description:
We are looking for a motivated Software Engineer to join our growing team. You will be responsible for analyzing business requirements, designing software solutions, and writing clean, scalable code. 

Key Responsibilities:
- Analyze user requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Write clean, testable, and efficient code (JavaScript, Python, or Java).
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features.
- Troubleshoot, debug, and upgrade existing software.

Requirements:
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 1-2 years of experience in software development or analysis.
- Strong understanding of object-oriented programming and web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React or Node.js).
- Experience with databases (SQL or MongoDB).
- Familiarity with Git and version control.

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS or Azure).
- Knowledge of Agile/Scrum methodologies.
- Strong problem-solving skills and excellent communication.
